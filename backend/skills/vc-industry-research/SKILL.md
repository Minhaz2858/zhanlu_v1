# VC Industry Research

Generate professional primary-market / venture capital industry research reports — sector deep dives, investment memos, and market analysis.

## When to use

- "Analyze this industry / sector deep dive / market report"
- PE/VC style research documents (Chinese or English)
- Sectors: TMT, consumer, healthcare, industrials (or any primary-market vertical)
- "投资分析", "行业研究", "赛道分析", "sector map", "investment memo"

## Report structure

1. **Cover** — sector, region, period, report type (sector deep dive / market map / investment memo), date
2. **Executive summary / 核心观点** — the investment-relevant answer first: sector attractiveness, key themes, where value accrues
3. **Sector overview** — definition, value chain, size, growth, maturity, cyclicality
4. **Market map / 赛道图谱** — segments and players positioned on axes (e.g. maturity × growth, or price × performance)
5. **Key trends** — technology, demand, policy, capital-flow trends, each with evidence and implications
6. **Segment deep dives** — for each attractive segment: market size, growth, competitive structure, unit economics, barriers, representative companies
7. **Competitive analysis** — incumbents vs startups, market share shifts, defensibility
8. **Investment themes & opportunities** — ranked: theme | evidence | timing | capital intensity | risk
9. **Risk analysis** — market, technology, regulatory, execution risks
10. **Valuation & comparables** (for memos) — comp table, funding history, valuation benchmarks
11. **Appendix** — data tables, sources, glossary

## VC framing rules

- Always answer "why now" and "where does value accrue" — VC research is decision-oriented
- Separate market facts from opinions; label estimates
- Company mentions must be factual (funding, metrics as publicly known); never fabricate a startup's traction
- For fake-data/simulation requests, label all figures "illustrative"

## Output

PDF or DOCX report by default; PPTX for board/investment-committee presentation. Chinese or English per the user's language.
