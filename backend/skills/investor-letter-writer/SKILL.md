# Investor Letter Writer

Draft classic investment memos and investor letters in the style of top VC deal memos and institutional investor communications.

## When to use

- "Write an investment memo / deal memo / IC memo"
- "Draft an investor letter / LP update"
- "Analyze this company/startup/theme and document the thesis"
- Fundraising updates, quarterly LP letters, internal investment committee documentation

## Memo structure (deal memo)

1. **Header block** — company, sector, stage, round size, valuation, lead, date, memo author
2. **Recommendation** — the ask in one line: invest/don't invest, amount, structure
3. **Thesis** — 3-5 reasons this is a compelling investment (specific, testable)
4. **Company overview** — what they do, business model, unit economics, go-to-market
5. **Market** — size, growth, structure, why now
6. **Product & technology** — differentiation, defensibility, moat analysis
7. **Traction & metrics** — real numbers where available: revenue, growth, retention, CAC/LTV, gross margin
8. **Team** — founders, backgrounds, why they're the right team, gaps
9. **Competition** — landscape, positioning, alternatives
10. **Risks** — ranked: execution, market, competitive, regulatory, financing
11. **Valuation & structure** — round math, cap table impact, downside protection
12. **Decision & next steps** — proposed terms, diligence checklist, follow-ups

## Investor letter structure (LP/portfolio update)

1. **Opening** — period, fund/portfolio status in 1-2 sentences
2. **Portfolio highlights** — wins, milestones, financing events
3. **Challenges** — honest underperformance with context and response
4. **Market commentary** — relevant macro/sector observations
5. **New investments / exits** — brief deal notes
6. **Fund metrics** — MOIC, TVPI, DPI, IRR (only real numbers)
7. **Forward outlook** — focus for next period
8. **Closing** — gratitude, availability

## Styling rules

- Terse, argument-first prose; a reader should get the thesis in 30 seconds
- Every claim about the business is labeled: fact, estimate, or assumption
- Numbers formatted consistently; no invented metrics — mark "to be confirmed via diligence" where unverified
- Keep memos 2-6 pages; letters 1-3 pages

## Output

DOCX or PDF by default. Offer PPTX only when the user asks for a deck version.
