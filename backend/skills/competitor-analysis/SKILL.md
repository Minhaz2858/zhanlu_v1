# Competitor Analysis

Analyze competitors' SEO and GEO strategies — ranking keywords, content, backlinks, and AI citations — to reveal concrete opportunities to outperform them.

## When to use

- "Analyze competitors / competitive SEO / competitive analysis"
- "Who ranks for <keyword>?"
- "What are my competitors doing?"
- "Why do they rank higher than us?"
- Any request about competitor keywords, backlinks, AI citations, or content gaps.

## Workflow

1. **Identify competitors** — from the user's message, their industry, or search results. If unknown, pick the 3-5 most relevant players in the same market/segment.
2. **Keyword landscape** — for the target topic/keywords, gather who ranks top 10 (organic), what queries they cover, and where the user's domain appears.
3. **Content audit** — for each competitor: page types, topic clusters, content depth, update frequency, format (blog/landing/video), and CTAs.
4. **Backlink / authority signal** — count referring domains, estimate authority tier, note which high-value domains link to competitors.
5. **AI citation / GEO check** — where does each competitor appear in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) for the target queries? What sources do those answers cite?
6. **Gap synthesis** — build a table: Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Keywords they win | Keywords we can take | Evidence.
7. **Opportunity ranking** — prioritize by (search demand × our ability to win × strategic value). Output concrete next actions.

## Output format

Always deliver a structured report:
- Executive summary (3-5 bullets: who leads, why, where the gap is)
- Competitive landscape table
- Keyword opportunity table (with estimated difficulty/volume where available)
- AI-citation findings
- Recommended actions (ranked, specific)

Ground every claim in the actual data gathered — never invent competitor metrics. When a figure cannot be verified, mark it "unverified" rather than guessing.

## Pitfalls

- Don't stop at SERP rankings — AI citations increasingly drive traffic; always include the GEO layer.
- Distinguish "competitor ranks because of content quality" vs "because of domain authority" — the fix differs.
- If the user names a niche, analyze that niche's real players, not generic industry leaders.
