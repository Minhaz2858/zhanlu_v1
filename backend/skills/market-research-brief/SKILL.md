# Market Research Brief

Generate professional consulting-style market insight reports with data-driven analysis, executive summaries, and strategic recommendations.

## When to use

- "Market analysis / industry analysis / market research"
- "Competitive landscape report"
- "Consumer behavior study"
- "TAM/SAM/SOM sizing", "market size and forecast"
- Strategic documents in a top-tier consulting visual style

## Report structure

1. **Cover** — title, market, region, period, report date, classification (Public/Confidential)
2. **Executive summary** — the answer first: market size, growth rate, key trends, top opportunities, strategic recommendation (5-7 bullets max)
3. **Scope & methodology** — definitions, regions covered, data sources, time horizon, assumptions
4. **Market definition & segmentation** — product/service categories, customer segments, geographic split
5. **Market size & forecast** — current size, CAGR, forecast with a chart; split by segment and region
6. **Key trends & drivers** — demand drivers, technology shifts, regulatory forces, each with evidence
7. **Competitive landscape** — market share, positioning map, competitor profiles (strengths/strategies/differentiation)
8. **Customer analysis** — buyer personas, needs, behavior, willingness to pay
9. **Opportunities & threats** — structured: opportunity | evidence | size | effort to capture
10. **Strategic recommendations** — 3-5 concrete recommendations, each tied to a finding
11. **Appendix** — data tables, sources, glossary

## Data rules

- Ground every figure in the connected datasource or cited public sources (with source + year)
- Never invent market numbers; where data is unavailable, mark clearly "illustrative / to be confirmed" and never present estimates as facts
- For explicit fake-data requests, label all figures "illustrative" on the cover and in methodology
- Prefer market data sources over company internal data when the user asks for a market view (see Zhanlu KB binding: market intent picks the market KB)

## Visual style

- Consulting style: clean tables, one chart per key claim, consistent color coding
- Charts: market size trend, segment split, competitive positioning
- Executive summary first; detail after; every recommendation traces to a finding

## Output

HTML report or DOCX. PPTX when a board-ready summary deck is requested.
