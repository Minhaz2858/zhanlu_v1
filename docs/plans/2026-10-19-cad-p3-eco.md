affected" — deterministic impact reports + a revision store with an explicit
human approval gate.

`cad_revision_create` (before/after snapshots → deterministic impact report,

component bounds — real interference needs the Fusion measure API, deferred);

after the user says so in conversation (full HITL approval-tool wiring later).
